Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Rahasya Lok Uploader ("the app") is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel रहस्य लोक (@RahasyaKaLok). It is used only by the channel owner.

What the app does

The app uploads Shorts videos created for the Rahasya Lok channel to that same channel, and sets their title, description, tags and publish time, using the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

The app uses Google OAuth with the YouTube upload permission for the channel owner's own account only. It does not access, collect, store or share any data about other users or viewers. It does not read comments, subscribers, analytics or any personal information.

Storage

The only stored credential is the channel owner's OAuth refresh token, kept encrypted as a secret in a private GitHub repository. No other data is stored.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through youtube.com/@RahasyaKaLok.
